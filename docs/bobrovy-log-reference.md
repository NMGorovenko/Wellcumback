# Bobrovy Log: simplified summer landmark

The model uses an original mesh, without photographic textures. References were inspected on 2026-09-24 in the Codex in-app browser.

## Sources and observed features

- [Official summer map](https://bobrovylog.ru/karta/?s=leto): a forecourt/parking strip at the foot of wooded slopes, K1 to the left of the central service complex, K2 to its right, the circular Mirage complex farther right, branching cleared pistes and a winding Rodelbahn beside the right-hand pistes. This is an illustrated map, not a survey.
- [Official chairlift description](https://bobrovylog.ru/services/kanatno-kreselnaya-doroga/), also confirmed in [About the fanpark](https://bobrovylog.ru/about/): two four-seat chairlift lines. The rendering retains two complete cables with moving four-seat benches and open lower/upper terminals.
- [Oasis exterior in INRTU's account of its own visit](https://eng.istu.edu/news/news/inrtu-prof-artur-kharinsky-presented-archaeological-findings-at-siberian-historical-forum/), [photo](https://eng.istu.edu/upload/sprint.editor/dd3/dd3d62525577ae030bf66bc4f2e8d254.jpg): tall sloped glass entrance bay between stone fins, recessed doors, long low left wing, rounded right wing, light metal upper cladding, large unobstructed paved arrival area.
- [Official Mirage page](https://bobrovylog.ru/services/plyazhnyy-kompleks-mirazh/), [photo](https://bobrovylog.ru/upload/iblock/ee7/ee7d3ed7d450748ccc39b54bf9fbffd0.jpg): circular outdoor pool, blue water, surrounding low glazed enclosure, pale deck and parasols.

## Interpretation and scale

No building dimensions, building footprints or geographic coordinates were measured from these photographs. The building's facade composition, the circular pool and the relative order of the two lift lines are observed features. Mesh dimensions, piste widths/curves, tower spacing, forecourt extent, colors and Rodelbahn turns are art-directed approximations for the existing compressed city. They must not be described as a surveyed reconstruction.

The Oasis mesh occupies a 72 × 30 game-metre parcel centered at (-838, 1268), with a 16 m maximum silhouette. The separate Mirage parcel is 36 × 36 at (-742, 1276). Existing Sibirskaya access and the arrival stop/parking at (-838, 1224) lead onto a ground-following open forecourt. The two stations sit beyond this public arrival area, at (-902, 1282) and (-785, 1282). The larger real separation of K1 and K2, the valley and hillside have deliberately been shortened to preserve the driving map.

Architecture uses shared palette materials and participates in city material batching. The 32 chairs and at most 340 trees use instancing; the piste ribbons share one mesh. There are no new image textures or forests outside the bounded ski area. The forecourt has no fountain, barriers, random furniture or raised slabs obstructing the approach.

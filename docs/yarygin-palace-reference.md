# Ivan Yarygin Sports Palace: simplified exterior

The building occupying the existing `arena` parcel on Otdykha Island represents the Ivan Yarygin Sports Palace. The previous Platinum Arena name and generic cylindrical model were removed.

## References

- [Venue operator: Ivan Yarygin Sports Palace](https://www.krascsp.ru/o_centre/filiali/dvorec_sporta_imeni_ivana_yarigina/).
- [Official exterior photograph](https://www.krascsp.ru/static/uploaded/images/filials/base/img_8644.jpg).
- [Official exterior and forecourt photograph](https://www.krascsp.ru/static/uploaded/images/custom_publications/mosaic_image/base/img_86713.jpg).

The parent agent visually inspected these photos. Observed characteristics are the white/grey curving upper body, elevated central tip with a tall narrow glazed seam, round windows above the glazed ground-floor gallery, a broad low entrance canopy, and a foreground monument with a wrestler on a stone pedestal.

The original game mesh interprets those features with a flared elliptical shell and a shallow curved roof whose front/rear rim is higher than its sides. It is not a scan or a measured reconstruction. The rear roof continuation, exact curve, panel count, sculpture anatomy, dimensions and distances are simplified. Geometry remains inside the original 65 × 38 game-metre collision parcel and 12 m height; the monument occupies its front edge without extending onto the surrounding island road.

The hull and roof are parametric meshes. Portholes are instanced circles, wall joints and gallery elements participate in the existing material batching. One generated facade inscription identifies the palace; no photographs are copied into game textures.
